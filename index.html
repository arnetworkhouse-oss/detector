How Veridian works
Three precise steps. No manual work needed.
01
Upload your image
Drag and drop any image file. We support JPG, PNG, WEBP, AVIF, and HEIC up to 50MB. Files are processed in-memory and never stored permanently.
02
Deep neural analysis
Our multi-layer model inspects pixel-level inconsistencies, DCT frequency artifacts, noise patterns, and generative fingerprints that AI image models leave behind.
03
Receive a precision score
A clear AI probability score with confidence rating returns instantly. Soft green means authentic. Soft red indicates AI-generated. No ambiguity, no guesswork.
Capabilities
Built for serious use
Designed for journalists, researchers, legal teams, and content platforms.
Image Detection
Catches outputs from Midjourney, DALL·E, Stable Diffusion, Firefly, and Adobe Generative Fill with over 98% accuracy across formats.
Face & Portrait Detection
Specialized model for AI-generated faces — catches GAN portraits, diffusion-based headshots, and composite face swaps with high precision.
Instant Results
Analysis completes in under 3 seconds for most images. No waiting queues, no batch delays. Results render directly in the browser.
Privacy First
Files are analyzed in an isolated sandbox and purged immediately after. No content is stored, indexed, or used for model training.
REST API
Integrate detection directly into your platform via our clean REST API. SDKs available for Python, Node.js, and Go. Full docs included.
Detailed Reports
Export PDF or JSON reports with forensic breakdowns. Court-admissible format with confidence intervals and model attribution.
